# Fusione guidata — P1

## Optional native case panel

When the installed host exposes `vera_workspace_fusion_catalogue`, the optional
Fusione panel can reopen an explicitly host-bound existing multi-company case.
Keep the normal specialist intake, selected company Archives and existing case
grants. The panel neither initializes a substitute case nor discovers client
folders. It shows all accessible records and revisions, prepared proposal
identities, separate exact-version confirmations and verified exports.

For each verified export, the source panel displays the saved dossier and
workpapers under current company permissions. Fusione is outside Vera's
comparison-report scope. Reopening does not claim a new model phase or prove
what the model received. Shared runtime, ticket parsing, long identifier
wrapping and installed-host acceptance remain unqualified.

Prepared proposals use the unchanged public `put`, `workpaper`, `bind_archive`
or `import_archive` helpers. Review the complete preview before independently
confirming a write. Professional approval remains a separate action requiring
the actual case reviewer grant, stated role, defined scope and expressed
confirmation. Declared identities are not authenticated signatures. Combined
dossiers remain in the merger case; do not copy them into one company's Archive
or close engagements through the panel. Recover uncertain writes through the
ordinary specialist workflow rather than repeating them.

Initial native intake, model-led proposal authoring and installed-host acceptance
remain pending. The shared renderer and ticket parser have unresolved source
checks; this panel is not release-qualified. Continue the independent file-based
Codex/Cowork workflow whenever native routes are unavailable.
