# Validate Answer

[Source code](https://github.com/fabioannovazzi/app_files/tree/main/plugins/deep-research-validator) · [GNU AGPLv3 License](https://github.com/fabioannovazzi/app_files/blob/main/LICENSE)

Validate research reports, legal memos, one-page letters, and other
professional answers against their answer contract and available sources.
Codex reviews answer-contract conformance and material-claim coverage, then
assesses source identity, semantic support, reasoning, issue-specific treatment,
claim disposition, and professional-judgment boundaries. Fixed code captures
sources and validates observable facts and record shape without deciding claim
meaning.

Vera and Lucia also resolve the same `skills/deep-research/SKILL.md` method
from this component. Research alternates source investigation, semantic
validation and adversarial challenge with persistent issue/cycle records.
Distinct final reviews can reopen targeted research. `scripts/research_record.py`
binds the declared research record and exact reviewed artifacts; it does not
certify legal correctness or the quality of the stopping decision.

The delivered answer must equal the embedded reviewed document. Source
inspection preserves resource IDs and immutable original/text captures across
passes. Research record schema 2.0 resolves pinned evidence, reviewed effects
and dependencies; review schema 2.2 distinguishes quotations, paraphrases and
translations. `scripts/research_evaluation.py` retains executed version-bound
runs and answer-bound assessor records and exports comparisons with workflow
labels withheld. It performs no research or automatic quality assessment.
