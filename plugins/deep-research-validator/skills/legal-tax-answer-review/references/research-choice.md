# One shared research method

Vera and Lucia use their bundled `skills/deep-research/SKILL.md` for every new
legal, tax or compliance research assignment. Each wrapper resolves the same
method in `modules/deep-research-validator`. Read it completely after preparation.
Use `generation_route: codex_direct` and the current host model and tools.
There is no ordinary/deep quality choice, optional research-plugin dependency,
API credential request, model change or automatic external-account handoff.

Depth follows the material issues, conflicting evidence and remaining gaps.
A short answer can require extensive investigation. A straightforward question
can reach supported treatment quickly using the same method. Never impose a
token-saving shortcut, a fixed number of searches or a minimum report length.
Respect an explicit user limit and report the investigation it prevents.

Preparation-only requests remain preparation. Supplied-answer review uses
`generation_route: external_document`; do not regenerate the supplied position
or force a research choice. Research its material gaps when review requires it.
Older route labels in imported records describe their provenance; they do not
instruct this workflow to install or invoke a discontinued research plugin.

Missing tools, gated sources and unavailable current-law access are evidence
limits. Use available authorized sources, identify unfinished work and retain
the substantive review. Do not relabel a limited investigation as complete,
invent sources or move case material to another account. A user-requested
external research handoff is a separate explicit assignment, not a second
modality of this pipeline.

Continuous validation and challenges to provisional conclusions belong in every
research assignment. The distinct final opposing opinion still follows only
when `adversarial-scope.md` records `adversarial_policy: required`.
