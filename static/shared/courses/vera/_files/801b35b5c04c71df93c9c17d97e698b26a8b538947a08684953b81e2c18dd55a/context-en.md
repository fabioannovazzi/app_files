# Officina Arco

Entirely fictional case: Officina Arco, one Italian company, EUR. Prepare net-debt analysis at the statement date. All amounts are positive balances at the same date, not flows to add across months. Bank loan outstanding is remaining loan principal; Unrestricted bank cash is available bank cash. Restricted deposit is separate and remains restricted; Ordinary trade payables are routine supplier balances. For this exercise, the request is to include the loan and available cash and exclude the restricted deposit and trade payables. The professional must review and record this definition in the case; no approval is prefilled. No other debt, leases, guarantees, accrued interest, bank statements or sale agreement is supplied. Coverage is limited to this statement; there is no completeness assurance or purchase-price adjustment. English column names, ungrouped dot decimals. Practice uses March balances in a new calculation while preserving February.
