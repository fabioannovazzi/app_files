# Changed vehicle availability

New fictional evidence for practice: the supplier says the vehicle will not be available on the proposed afternoons. Saturday morning is possible but no revised quote is supplied. The three informal customer expressions of interest concerned weekday pickup; Saturday willingness has not been checked. No new volume, cost or price has been confirmed. Ask how this affects feasibility and the next test; do not automatically replace assumptions with invented figures.
