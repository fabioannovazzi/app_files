Your objective

Fictional Arco considers a subscription service with 100 average paying customers at EUR 50 per month and EUR 42,000 annual costs. The price is a team proposal; demand evidence consists of five exploratory interviews, not orders.

How the workflow works

Follow the three steps. Pause at the decision that changes the result.

  1. Define the decision and separate evidence, accepted assumptions and information still needed.
  2. Build the current plan with coherent customers, prices, costs and timing. Link assumptions to sources and distinguish economic results from cash.
  3. Compare useful scenarios and present the decision, sensitivities and evidence-gathering actions for professional review.
