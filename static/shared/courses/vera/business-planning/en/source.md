# One investment, two assumptions to test

Entirely fictional teaching case.

Fictional Arco considers a subscription service with 100 average paying customers at EUR 50 per month and EUR 42,000 annual costs. The price is a team proposal; demand evidence consists of five exploratory interviews, not orders.

| Fictional assumption | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Average paying customers | 100 | P1 · team assumption |
| Monthly price | EUR 50 | P1 · proposal |
| Annual costs | EUR 42,000 | P2 · operating estimate |
| Exploratory interviews | 5 | P3 · notes; no orders |

P1 and P2 are explicit assumptions. P3 explores needs without proving that 100 customers will subscribe.

A basic economic scenario. Revenue less these costs does not establish cash flow, tax or investment needs.
