How the workflow works

Follow the three steps. Pause at the decision that changes the result.

  1. Inspect exports and review roles, columns, categories, signs, currency, period and cutoff. Confirm control totals.
  2. Run the local pack and read metrics and limitations. Show absolute and percentage changes, withholding misleading percentages on zero or negative bases.
  3. Explain the main contributions while separating observations from hypotheses. Optional sections require adequate inputs and commentary remains reviewable.
