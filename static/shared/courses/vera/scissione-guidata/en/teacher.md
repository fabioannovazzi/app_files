## 1. When to use it · 45 s

Connect the function to a concrete professional task.

Prepare a versioned dossier, read its schedules and revisit decisions after a change.

Arco Meccanica S.r.l. transfers a division to newly formed Arco Servizi S.r.l. Anna and Bruno own 60% and 40%. Every name, document and amount is fictional.

Italian OIC, EUR, partial proportional demerger into a new beneficiary, homogeneous rights and no reciprocal holdings. Existing beneficiaries, scorporo, asymmetric assignments, IFRS and foreign cases are unsupported. The dossier does not certify legal validity, sign or file.
