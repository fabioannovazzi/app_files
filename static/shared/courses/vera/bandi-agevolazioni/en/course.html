03 / 75 s

How the workflow works

Follow the three steps. Pause at the decision that changes the result.

  1. Define the discovery objective and review relevant sources. Bind each opportunity to its exact source and version.
  2. For the selected business, compare every requirement with company evidence and identify missing documents.
  3. Prepare the opportunity sheet, checklist and reviewable dossier. Distinguish relevance, verified requirements and documentary readiness; do not submit the application.
