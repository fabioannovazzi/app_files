# An interesting opportunity is not yet an eligible application

Entirely fictional teaching case.

A wholly fictional call funds machinery for manufacturing businesses in one region. Arco proposes an EUR 80,000 investment. Evidence of the operating location and the date the expenditure started is missing.

| Fictional source | Condition or fact | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B1 · DEMO call § 2 | Operating unit in the call's region | Location not documented |
| B1 · § 4 | Expenditure starts after application | Order date missing |
| C1 · Arco project | Machinery: EUR 80,000 | Quotation supplied |

B1 defines only the example's conditions. C1 establishes a project, not satisfaction of every requirement. No grant percentage is invented.

The DEMO call does not exist and cannot receive applications. Real opportunities require current official sources.
