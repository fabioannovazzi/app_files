How the workflow works

Follow the three steps. Pause at the decision that changes the result.

  1. Observe the site and define the objective, authorized scope and conditions that must stop the procedure.
  2. Prepare the capability from actual observed elements. Test on the local site and preserve evidence of steps and results.
  3. Rerun the authorized step, open the downloaded file and compare it with the expected content. Review the capability if the page or controls change.
