# Connect each purchase invoice to the actual posting

Entirely fictional teaching case.

The fictional purchase batch has three invoices: one with a consistent posting, one with no posting found and one with two ambiguous candidates. Both the period and accounting population are explicitly bounded.

| Fictional invoice | Available accounting support | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PA-101 · EUR 1,220 | One consistent entry | Review nature and treatment |
| PA-102 · EUR 610 | No candidate in the period | Recover or clarify |
| PA-103 · EUR 244 | Two candidates | Resolve identity |

P1 is the XML batch and G1 the period's posting export. Findings stay bound to both perimeters; no missing posting is invented.

Review of supplied purchase invoices and postings. A missing link in this perimeter does not prove an invoice was never booked.
