How the workflow works

Follow the three steps. Pause at the decision that changes the result.

  1. Import the invoice population and actual postings; qualify period, accounts, identities and batch totals.
  2. Run the current matching and review method, including its required native capabilities. Leave unavailable stages pending instead of simulating them.
