01

When to use it

Officina Arco supplies three XML invoices and their March postings. The practice wants to identify points requiring examination.

First use of Italian purchase-invoice review. Vera reads invoices and ledger, compares them and assesses account consistency; it does not change postings.

02

Files and request

Open ledger-march.csv, the invoices folder and context-en.md. Supply invoices and actual ledger entries for the same period. Vera checks the columns and prepares their mapping for review before execution.

Vera, review these purchase invoices against Officina Arco’s March postings. Show me what needs attention in the exception workbook and explain why.
03

Run the workflow

  1. The working chat checks the batch and ledger columns. Voice explains invoice, movement and booked account identity and helps resolve material ambiguities.
  2. The current workflow matches invoices to movements, checks amounts and uses its native reviewer to assess descriptions and accounts. Voice follows actual progress and distinguishes calculation from judgement.
  3. Open the “Exceptions” worksheet in the Excel file first. Choose a row, read the reason for review and trace it to the invoice description and account actually used. Review findings before drawing conclusions.
During the lesson, the working chat runs the function and produces the result. If a step is unavailable, explain what is missing and keep the lesson incomplete.
04

Use the result

  1. Excel exception workbook with reasons and references to examine.
  2. Batch summary and comparison trail back to invoices and ledger entries.

Check batch coverage and whether documents support each finding. “No issue detected” is a screening outcome, not certification. Decisions to change accounts or correct entries follow the normal accounting process.

05

Pause and check

  1. Before execution, identify the two sets of evidence being compared.
  2. In the result, explain a finding using its invoice and posting.

These pauses help you learn how to use the function. They are not a technical detail quiz.

06
