How to use this function

Assess company arrangements with Vera

Learn to request an assessment of company arrangements, supply evidence of their operation and use findings and proposed actions.

The explanation and a short practice take about 5–8 minutes. Processing and your questions can extend the session.

Start this course

  1. Open Codex on your computer with Vera installed and select @Vera in a chat.
  2. Copy the request below, paste it into the chat and send it.
  3. Turn on Codex voice in this chat. The assistant will guide you to open the second working chat in another window: listen and ask questions here, and watch the work and results there.

The fictional lesson files are already included in the plugin. You can also take the lesson in writing. This button copies the request; the lesson starts when you send it in Codex.

Two chats, one lesson

Speak with the teacher using the standard Codex voice. In the working chat, open in the window beside it, the function runs the case using the prepared files and shows its actual results. The teacher follows those results: interrupt, ask questions and change the pace whenever you need.

01

When to use it

Officina Arco wants to review responsibilities, monthly closing and collection information. We have its company profile, a procedure and one operating example in the fictional case.

An initial focused assessment of organizational, administrative and accounting arrangements in an Italian company. Scope follows the documents; this is not a whole-company certificate.

02

Files and request

Open company-en.md, procedure-en.md and operation-en.md. State company, assignment and date: 31 March 2026. Provide operating examples alongside written procedures.

Vera, assess Officina Arco’s arrangements for responsibilities, monthly closing and collection information. Use these documents and show the assessment, limits and actions to discuss.
03

Run the workflow

  1. In the working chat, Vera reconstructs how the company operates and proposes scope. Voice explains why both a procedure and an example of its use are useful.
  2. Vera compares responsibilities, deadlines, deliveries and decisions, verifies relevant professional sources and prepares proportionate findings. Voice follows actual evidence and questions that could change the assessment.
  3. Open the generated memo. Read the overall assessment first, then one finding, its source and linked action. Distinguish proposed owners and timing from accepted commitments.
During the lesson, the working chat runs the function and produces the result. If a step is unavailable, explain what is missing and keep the lesson incomplete.
04

Use the result

  1. Memo with reasoned assessment, observations, findings and improvement plan.
  2. Source-linked record for subsequent assessment and decisions actually expressed.

Check the link between assessment and evidence, scope, unassessed areas and proportionate actions. A written policy or one successful cycle does not prove continuity; a documentation gap alone does not prove the process fails.

05

Pause and check

  1. Before analysis, identify the process and one available operating example.
  2. In the memo, link an action to the finding and source that justify it.

These pauses help you learn how to use the function. They are not a technical detail quiz.

06

Try it yourself

Add update-en.md to the same file and request review at 30 April. Vera links the newly generated record, reassesses every earlier action and preserves the first version. Explain how the new evidence changes the assessment.
