# New April evidence

Fictional update at 30 April 2026. Sara is absent from 7 to 18 April. Elena sends March documents on 21 April; the summary arrives on 28 April and Elena’s review is not yet recorded. The procedure’s dispatch and reporting deadlines were missed in this cycle. Paolo collected some invoices during the absence but had no access to the due-date list. No substitute has been formally agreed. Availability of the April-delivered report does not prove process effectiveness or completion of earlier actions.
