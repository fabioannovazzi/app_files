How to use this function

Prepare a new client with Vera

Learn to turn first-contact information into a client-startup dossier for the practice to review and complete.

The explanation and a short practice take about 5–8 minutes. Processing and your questions can extend the session.

Start this course

  1. Open Codex on your computer with Vera installed and select @Vera in a chat.
  2. Copy the request below, paste it into the chat and send it.
  3. Turn on Codex voice in this chat. The assistant will guide you to open the second working chat in another window: listen and ask questions here, and watch the work and results there.

The fictional lesson files are already included in the plugin. You can also take the lesson in writing. This button copies the request; the lesson starts when you send it in Codex.

Two chats, one lesson

Speak with the teacher using the standard Codex voice. In the working chat, open in the window beside it, the function runs the case using the prepared files and shows its actual results. The teacher follows those results: interrupt, ask questions and change the pace whenever you need.

01

When to use it

Officina Arco requests ongoing accounting support. Starting from the interview form, we prepare a dossier covering parties, requested services, documents to obtain and decisions still needed.

Onboarding an Italian client. The result is an internal professional-review dossier, including document and monitoring plans. Signing and activating the relationship follow the practice’s normal process.

02

Files and request

Open profile-en.md. It records reported facts about the company, representative, ownership and services. It is already organized and does not come from an approved document-preparation run; Vera must record it as direct evidence.

  • profile-en.md
Vera, prepare Officina Arco’s new-client dossier from this form. Organize the facts, proposed engagement, checks to perform and documents to request.
03

Run the workflow

  1. Vera opens the teaching case and links the form to the new client. When starting from an unorganized folder, the same function also includes document preparation.
  2. The current workflow organizes parties and services, prepares proposals and missing-information requests, and compiles the dossier. The voice chat follows these steps and distinguishes reported facts, checks and decisions.
  3. Open the dossier’s review page. Start with client facts and the engagement, then documents to obtain and decisions to complete. The memo and other attachments retain the supporting checks.
During the lesson, the working chat runs the function and produces the result. If a step is unavailable, explain what is missing and keep the lesson incomplete.
04

Use the result

  1. Review page and case facts: give the practice an organized basis for the interview, with client, engagement and sources.
  2. Missing-information request, document plan and monitoring plan: identify subsequent work and matters still requiring confirmation.

Compare parties, ownership and services with the form. Identity, AML, privacy and terms require normal professional checks; reported facts are not already verified.

05

Pause and check

  1. Before starting, identify the client and requested services.
  2. At delivery, find a source, a document to obtain and a decision still needed.

These pauses help you learn how to use the function. They are not a technical detail quiz.

06

Try it yourself

Add contact-update-en.md and request a new dossier version. Sara becomes the administrative document contact; Elena remains the named representative.
  • profile-en.md
  • contact-update-en.md

The new dossier includes the contact and update source, preserves the preceding version and does not grant representation powers that were not reported.

Supply the new client’s information and documents, state the requested services and use the dossier to complete checks, terms and review before starting the relationship.

The kit contains fictional files and a prepared outline. Demonstration and practice results come from fresh runs of the current function.

The lesson library, profile and progress stay on your computer. They are not sent to Mparanza. Voice and content read in chat are processed by your OpenAI account: local storage does not mean offline inference.
