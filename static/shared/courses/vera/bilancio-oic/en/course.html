03 / 75 s

How the workflow works

Follow the three steps. Pause at the decision that changes the result.

  1. Confirm year, statement form, principles and language. Inspect sources and preserve each balance's locator.
  2. Propose a reasoned mapping and resolve ambiguous items. Keep decisions awaiting review visible.
  3. Use the current contract and taxonomy for checks and generation. Deliver drafts and diagnostics with their review status, without claiming filing.

The check that matters

Before revealing the answer, say what you would check.

Balanced totals do not certify classification, completeness or compliance. Drafts retain their limitations and professional-review requirement.

Can we assign all other receivables to one category just to finish mapping?

Compare your reasoning

No. The nature of the balance needs supporting detail. Keep the point open and submit the proposal for professional review.
