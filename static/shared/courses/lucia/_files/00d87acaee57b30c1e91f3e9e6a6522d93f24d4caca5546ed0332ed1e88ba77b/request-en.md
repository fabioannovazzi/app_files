# Client request — fictional case

Beta Laboratorio Srl asks Studio Esempio to prepare a new matter concerning a commercial dispute with Gamma Forniture Srl. Its contact is Elena Esempio, director. On 2 March 2026 Beta ordered 100 components for EUR 6,000, for delivery on 10 March. It paid a EUR 3,000 advance. The delivery record lists 80 components. On 12 March Beta requested the missing 20; on 13 March Gamma replied that it would check its warehouse. The firm must organize the documents and identify what to review before accepting the engagement. Stated jurisdiction: Italy; pre-litigation stage. The client reports no court service or procedural deadline. The teaching register covers only the three listed matters; searching it is not a complete firm conflict check. No professional decisions on conflicts, engagement, deadlines or opening have been supplied. All people and companies are fictional.
