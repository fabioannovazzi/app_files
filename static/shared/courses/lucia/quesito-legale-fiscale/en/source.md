# From a broad question to a verifiable answer

Entirely fictional teaching case.

A fictional client asks whether a cost is deductible. An invoice describes a service, but entity, period, regime and actual use are unknown. This lesson frames the question and answer scope without giving a tax opinion.

| Fictional element | Available | To clarify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invoice Q1 | Service description and amount | Actual use |
| Client question | Interest in deduction | Entity, regime and period |
| Legal sources | Not selected in this small case | Applicable framework after clarification |

Q1 supports a documentary description, not the tax conditions. Missing facts can change both research and conclusion.

A lesson on research and review method, not a substantive tax rule. A real case requires current applicable primary sources.
