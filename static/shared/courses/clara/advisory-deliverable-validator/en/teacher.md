## 1. When to use it · 45 s

Connect the function to a concrete professional task.

Learn to check whether an advisory document answers its assignment and whether its recommendation follows from the available evidence.

An external memo recommends launching Ciclo Arco’s daily collection service. We will review the whole short memo against the owner’s request and source notes.

A supplied Markdown memo and local evidence. Its original drafting history is unavailable, so the review matches claims to evidence now supplied.

## 2. Files and request · 60 s

Clara, review this memo against the assignment and supplied notes. Explain whether it is usable, what needs correction and which decisions remain with the owner.

## 3. Run the workflow · 105 s

Explain the step happening now and wait for its actual result.

Clara establishes the review contract, preserves the memo and prepares an inventory of its contents and the selected evidence.

The current review workflow examines scope, claims, reasoning, recommendation, limitations and readiness. The voice thread connects findings to the exact memo passages and source notes.

Open the review package. Read the overall delivery status, material findings and proposed corrections. The original stays intact; a revised memo requires its own review.

During the lesson, the working chat runs the function and produces the result. If a step is unavailable, explain what is missing and keep the lesson incomplete.

## 4. Use the result · 75 s

Open the document just produced and show where to start reading.

Readable review package: overall conclusion, source comparisons, corrections and remaining uncertainty.

Validation audit and inventories: what was reviewed and whether the review record is complete.

Compare the daily-service recommendation with actual customer statements and vehicle availability. A complete review record does not itself approve the memo or prove its claims.

## 5. Pause and check · 45 s

Make these checks at the indicated points during the work.

Before reviewing, distinguish the deliverable from its supporting evidence.

At delivery, explain the readiness conclusion and find the passage and source behind a material finding.

These pauses help you learn how to use the function. They are not a technical detail quiz.
