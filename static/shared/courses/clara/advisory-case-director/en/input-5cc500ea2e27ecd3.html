# New operational note — fictional exercise
The owner clarifies that the shop vehicle will not be available during the period originally planned for the trial. A neighbouring workshop is willing to discuss lending a vehicle, but terms, availability and costs have not been agreed.
Update the assessment using this information. Do not treat the loan as available or make bookings or contacts.
