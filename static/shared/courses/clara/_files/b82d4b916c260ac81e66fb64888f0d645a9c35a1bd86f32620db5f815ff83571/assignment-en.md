# Fictional assignment: bicycle collection service
The owner of Ciclo Arco wants to decide whether to trial bicycle collection and return for repairs. Compare a small appointment-based pilot with launching a daily service immediately. Audience: the owner. Requested output: a short memo with a recommendation, reasons, limitations and next step.
Use only the supplied customer and operational notes. Do not contact customers, research external data or commit spending. Prices, costs, willingness to pay and wider demand are not documented: do not produce a financial forecast.
Which option can we assess from this evidence? What should we verify before expanding the service?
