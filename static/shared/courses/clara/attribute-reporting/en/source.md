# An assortment signal is not market share

Entirely fictional teaching case.

A fictional North retailer package contains 20 new products, 12 refillable, and 80 products in the rest cohort, 24 refillable. The snapshot has no sales or market-share measures.

| Fictional cohort | Refillable products | Total products |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New | 12 | 20 |
| Rest | 24 | 80 |

A1 defines cohorts and the case attribute. Comparing 12 with 24 without their different denominators would mislead.

New-versus-rest comparison on a local teaching package. No server acquisition or fresh retailer scrape occurs during the lesson.
