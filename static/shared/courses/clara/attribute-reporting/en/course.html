How the workflow works

Follow the three steps. Pause at the decision that changes the result.

  1. Start from a coherent checked package, review attributes against products and preserve the contracted comparisons and thresholds.
  2. Interpret tables without promoting an attribute match to automatic judgment. Link product evidence and images to the package.
  3. Author the local report and complete independent semantic review, browser QA and current correctness checks. State the actual verdict and limitations.

The check that matters

Before revealing the answer, say what you would check.

Retain denominators and snapshot date. A product name or single attribute does not establish market success.

Does 60% mean refillable products generate 60% of sales?

Compare your reasoning

No. It is the share of new products with that attribute in the package. No sales measure is supplied.
