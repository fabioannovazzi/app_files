How to use this function

Prepare a business plan with Clara

Learn to start from a proposal, supply available information and obtain a report that helps decide what to test, change or investigate.

The explanation and a short practice take about 5–8 minutes. Processing and your questions can extend the session.

01

When to use it

Ciclo Arco is considering bicycle pickup and return. A proposal, economic assumptions and operating information are available, with important points still unverified.

A first business plan for a pilot decision. The report covers proposition, customers, operations, economics, cash, alternatives and next actions; missing information remains explicit.

02

Files and request

Open proposal-en.md, economics-en.md, operations-en.md and pilot-economics.csv. Explain the idea and decision and provide what you have. You need neither a complete model nor technical configuration files.

Clara, prepare a business plan to assess this bicycle pickup service. Use the proposal and assumptions, distinguish them from evidence and show what should be tested before launching the pilot.
03

Run the workflow

  1. In the working chat, Clara reads the proposal and clarifies customer, need and decision. Voice explains the planning process and which information helps answer the question.
  2. Clara assesses demand, operations, costs and alternatives, calculates supported economics and builds the report through the current workflow. Voice distinguishes evidence, assumptions and gaps without inventing missing cash costs.
  3. Open the generated HTML report. Start with the recommendation, move to economics and operations and trace a figure to its source. Read the cash limitations and the proposed test before making commitments.
During the lesson, the working chat runs the function and produces the result. If a step is unavailable, explain what is missing and keep the lesson incomplete.
04

Use the result

  1. One readable HTML report with recommendation, reasoning, available calculated figures and next actions.
  2. Sources and assumptions accessible in the report, with provisional status and matters for review.

Check that the recommendation follows from evidence, informal interest does not become proven demand, and partial economics are not presented as complete funding needs. Assumptions and conclusions remain subject to review.

05

Pause and check

  1. Before work starts, identify the decision the business plan should inform.
  2. In the report, find the recommendation, one dependency and the next useful test.

These pauses help you learn how to use the function. They are not a technical detail quiz.

06

Try it yourself

Add update-en.md to the same case and ask how the changed vehicle availability affects the plan. Clara revises recommendation, assumptions and required tests in a new report linked to the earlier one. Identify what changes even without new figures.

You can start a business plan with available information, read its recommendation and revise it when new evidence arrives.

Describe the business, customers, decision and constraints to Clara; provide documents and assumptions. Use the report to discuss the next step and return to the same case when prices, demand, operations or financing change.
