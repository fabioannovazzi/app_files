# Adeguati assetti: assessment and construction course

The existing five-language course now covers the current native assessment and
construction contracts. Its fictional Officina Arco case retains the original
31 March assessment, February operating example and 30 April follow-up. It adds
localized discovery/construction and practice chapters, unapproved raw facts,
and a separate financial lab. Revision: `2026-10-01.1`; package candidate:
Vera `0.1.303`. This document does not claim merge, deployment or publication.

The six-stage, 390-second outline is the introduction. Construction, operating
review and finance continue in resumable sessions; no measured full-course
learner duration is claimed. Codex retains the native teacher/worker journey;
Cowork retains the existing written, single-conversation course surface.

## What the learner does

1. Execute the scoped assessment and trace a finding to its source and action.
2. Collect attributed originals with one contextual question at a time, use the
   no-network HTML form, export Markdown before closing, import exact originals
   and resume the same practice with its sources and snapshot.
3. Qualify evidence and compare unknown/base, reasoned professional choice and
   target. The experimental STUDIO-AA-0.1 scale is not UNI certification or an
   adequacy verdict. N/A, material contradictions and missing information stay
   visible; unknown never becomes zero.
4. Link evidence, risk/finding, control and action. Design one proportionate
   monthly-close procedure and export actual Word/PDF/Markdown/JSON, editable
   CSV registers and a hash manifest.
5. Distinguish draft, explicit professional review, exact company adoption,
   execution evidence and operating review. Preserve versions and stale
   dependent decisions. A manual and a simulated cycle do not establish
   operating effectiveness or complete an operating action.
6. Reuse Business Planning v3 in a separate fictional financial lab, review exact
   calculation-ID mappings and reconciled totals, retain frozen Budget and later
   Forecast, and version KPI formulas/populations and observations. Unreviewed
   or partial plans, stale claims and unreconciled mappings remain blocked.

## Data handling

No new external route is introduced. The offline form embeds case data and keeps
answers in memory; it has no network, telemetry, localStorage or audio capture.
Returning the original Markdown lets its contents and selected company sources,
manuals, decisions and financial/KPI artifacts enter the selected account's
model context. There is no automatic anonymization or local-inference guarantee.
Teaching profile, progress and feedback remain local; no tutorial receipt or
change request is sent to the server. The construction workstream boundary is
unchanged; the affected teaching and version-bound service fingerprints were
reviewed and refreshed.
