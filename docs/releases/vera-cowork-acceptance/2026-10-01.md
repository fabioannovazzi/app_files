## Inferred

The bank workflow executes and writes usable native records for this simple,
reference-backed March case. Delivery is not accepted: the course source link
fails and promised deliverables cannot be opened from the response. Fresh-session
archive discovery worked in this one March case, while delivery still failed.
The review records mark both demo steps `failed`; the release gate remains red.
No public course-completion statement is justified.
