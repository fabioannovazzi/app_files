## Lesson and source material

Authoring lives in `scripts/course_materials/kits_<language>_fusione.json` and
`inputs/fusione/`. Native assets are generated under
`plugins/vera/assets/courses/fusione-guidata/`. The public guides are generated
under `static/shared/courses/vera/fusione-guidata/<language>/course.html`; they
link the original fictional inputs and a readable localized source note.
Existing Codex voice/two-chat and Cowork written/single-chat entry prompts apply.
The prepared-course specialist notes explain the actual two-company archive
route and outer tutorial run; no professional profile is required to start.

The demonstration uses independent Italian OIC companies. The practice uses
direct wholly owned incorporation, then introduces a separate changed creditor
balance into that same practice case. Both packs reuse current P1 synthetic-case
facts, with company-specific archive provenance and Beta calendar anchors.
They ship source facts and declared accounting/calendar hypotheses only, with
no precomputed report, approved request, reviewer confirmation or result specimen.

The lesson covers document selection and receipt/byte verification, professional
branch and valuation review, exact exchange/shareholder allocations, accounting
reconciliation and the draft opening journal, distinct tax bases, versioned
source-bound event calendars, an evidence-bound review dossier and selective
reopening after a change. Missing mandate, minutes, contracts, workforce,
litigation, tax decisions and execution receipts remain explicit gaps.

## Acceptance boundaries

Native regression prose and confirmations are explicitly synthetic fixtures.
The learner's actual decisions must be obtained in a live lesson; `demo-p1`
must not substitute for that lesson. Scripted checks establish software/input
behavior, not professional validation of a real merger or learner comprehension.
The component's workpaper headings and machine fields retain their current
language contract; localized guidance is not a promise of fully translated
professional reports. The standard 5–8 minute outline is explanation/short
practice pacing; processing, decisions and questions can extend the session.

Only ordinary independent and direct wholly owned domestic OIC incorporation
are taught. Advanced branches, signatures, accounting-system posting and filings
remain outside scope. Calendar periods are explicit fictional hypotheses, not
a pre-approved current legal rule pack. A real case requires current applicable
primary sources, evidenced facts and actual exact-version professional review.
Native voice/window behavior, installed-host acceptance, deployment and exact
Marketplace Published status require separate verification.
