Clara helps consultants plan advisory assignments as reviewable contracts, routes them to the right workflow, and validates deliverables against contract and evidence. She organizes documents, interviews and data, surfaces gaps and contradictions, and analyzes markets, customers, products, competitors and operations.

She turns the work into presentations, reports and reviewable workpapers. She also creates research videos from scenes and grounded scripts. Retailer Signals and Brand Fit compare retailer requirements with a brand’s current presence and product catalogue.

Sources, assumptions, unresolved questions and decisions remain visible. Professional judgement stays with the consultant.
