Clara helps consultants prepare commercial due diligence and other advisory work. She organizes evidence from documents, interviews and data, highlights gaps and contradictions, and analyzes markets, customers, products, competitors and operations. She turns the work into presentations, reports and reviewable workpapers, while Retailer Signals and Brand Fit help assess retailer requirements, a brand's current presence and its product catalogue. Sources, assumptions and open questions remain visible; the consultant keeps professional judgement.
