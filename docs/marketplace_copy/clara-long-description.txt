Clara helps consultants structure commercial due diligence and other advisory work. She organizes evidence from documents, interviews and data, surfaces gaps and contradictions, and analyzes markets, customers, products, competitors and operations.

She turns the work into presentations, reports and reviewable workpapers. She also creates narrated research videos from approved visual scenes and source-grounded scripts. Retailer Signals and Brand Fit compare retailer requirements with a brand’s current presence and product catalogue.

Sources, assumptions, unresolved questions and decisions remain visible. Professional judgement stays with the consultant.
