# Vera assetti: verification of Francesco's claims and delivery plan

Date: 2026-09-07. Status: researched proposal for implementation and a pilot.
No production change, client pilot, deployment or publication is claimed.

## Decision proposed

Proceed with a focused improvement to the existing assessment and follow-up
workflow. Test a separate, assisted initial-design path with one real company
before presenting that path as a released capability.

The useful professional task is to establish how responsibilities and controls
work, prepare targeted improvements, and later examine evidence of use. The
product should start with the company's actual activity and existing material.
It should not start by demanding a full questionnaire or producing a folder of
standard policies.

### Catanzaro, 6 February 2024, RG 2034/2023

Source: https://www.ilcaso.it/giurisprudenza/archivio/30745.pdf

Pages 7–9: the court discusses actual accounting/credit-management weaknesses,
the preventive role of arrangements even before crisis, and orders an inspection.
The illustrative inspection questions include an updated organization chart,
responsibilities, risk monitoring, forecasts, reporting and receivables procedures.
Further determinations are reserved pending inspection.

Supports attention to concrete processes and current arrangements. Does not
establish a universal employee-questionnaire requirement, a fixed document pack,
or automatic liability whenever a document is absent. It is an inspection decree,
not a final damages judgment.

### Venezia, 26 August 2025

Source: https://www.ilcaso.it/giurisprudenza/archivio/33569.pdf

Pages 9–12, inspected as page images: the court considers warning signs, board
responses and the absence of concrete strategies; it orders an inspection that
includes arrangements, group exposures and prospective financial sustainability.
The text distinguishes potentially deficient information arrangements from
management failing to draw timely consequences from existing warnings.

Supports reviewing information, decisions and action over time. Does not establish
that a digital archive or questionnaire by itself proves effectiveness, nor decide
that all similarly situated directors are liable. The inspection's factual and
procedural scope must be preserved.

## Intended first user and scope

First pilot: a commercialista working with one small operating company whose
management and relevant staff will participate. Prefer a case with accessible
records and a normal operating cycle, not an emergency insolvency assignment.
This is a pilot selection choice, not a rule excluding distressed firms from
assessment. Do not infer that Francesco has agreed to participate or supply data.

Focus initially on responsibilities and one or two relevant processes, such as
receivables and payments/accounting handoffs. Select them from actual risks,
not a universal template. Explain explicitly that a process pilot does not
establish overall adequacy of the enterprise's arrangements.

## Proposed user journey

1. **Understand current work.** Read existing documents and a recent operating
   example. Establish who prepares information, who decides and who follows up.
   Ask only questions whose answers affect the assessment or design.
2. **Return a concrete assessment.** Present strengths, gaps and unknowns with
   their sources. Distinguish documented arrangements, reported practice and
   corroborated operation. Explain why a gap matters to this company.
3. **Prepare targeted proposals.** Where requested in the initial-design pilot,
   draft a concise responsibility sheet and the necessary procedure or calendar.
   Use real people/roles and existing systems. Leave powers, ownership and dates
   as proposals until confirmed. Do not duplicate an existing register by default.
4. **Record management decisions.** Retain approved changes and actual dates.
   Approval is distinct from adoption, and adoption from observed operation.
5. **Review a real cycle.** At a professionally chosen interval, import relevant
   new evidence, examine exceptions and update findings. Show changes since the
   prior review and every outstanding action. This is user-initiated follow-up;
   a recorded review date does not create an automation.

The professional-facing result should be one readable assessment with its action
plan and, when requested, the few operational drafts required. Supporting source
records remain available through the archive. The client should not have to
understand JSON, hashes or plugin modules.

## Evidence handling

For a material event, distinguish the event date, document date, claimed operation
period, receipt/import date and review date. Unknown dates remain unknown.
Retain the source and applicable procedure version, responsible role, information
recipient, decision, resulting action and contradictory evidence where relevant.

Example: a September upload of a procedure dated March cannot, by itself, show
that staff followed it in April. An April register and a contemporaneous decision
may support a narrower conclusion. An employee's recollection is additional
reported evidence, not retroactive operating proof.

Hashes and archive records help preserve the reviewed bytes and the history of
the review. They do not independently authenticate source content, certify a legal
timestamp, establish completeness, or make a generated narrative contemporaneous.

## Delivery stages and decision gates

### A. Improve assessment and follow-up

Implement the five areas agreed in the source review: coverage, risk/control
analysis, chronology, conditional cooperative coverage and management review brief.
Reuse the existing module rather than introducing another top-level function.

The inspected v1 contract already has observations, findings, actions, source
hashes, `previous`, `changes_since_previous`, `prior_action_review` and an explicit
professional decision. First test the richer method within those structures.
Add optional structured event/control fields only if needed to deliver reliable
comparison, filtering or reporting. Do not change schemas merely to restate prose.

Gate: representative assessments satisfy the source-review scenarios, retain
contrary evidence and source/date distinctions, and save/reopen with the same
findings and prior-action links. Evaluate generated content, not just field presence.

### B. Run a supervised initial-design pilot

Use one consenting real company and one complete operating cycle. Management and
the commercialista review the drafts; involved staff try the agreed procedure.
Someone other than the author should review the resulting assessment and records.

Record professional minutes spent on intake, drafting, corrections and review;
client/staff time; material corrections; duplicate data entry; successful and failed
use; exceptions discovered; and what management changed. Compare with an actual
manual engagement or a documented estimate, clearly distinguishing the two.

Gate: staff can use the procedure without the author explaining every step,
the professional finds no material invented facts/powers, and the follow-up can
reconstruct actual use and unresolved failures. A polished draft alone does not pass.

### C. Decide the initial-design release scope

If A works but B fails, release only the assessment enhancement. If B demonstrates
useful assisted design, define its supported inputs, exclusions and outputs and
test at least a contrasting company before claiming broad applicability.

Measure whether the scope can support Francesco's indicative economics; do not
promise half-day delivery or adopt his price in advance. Evaluate whether clients
will provide follow-up evidence and pay for the service, not merely like a demo.

After implementation and required checks, update the explanation page to describe
only verified behavior and complete the normal package/release/deployment process.
Marketplace publication remains a separate release outcome.

## Conditions for stopping or narrowing

- If intake requires extensive organizational consulting, price/scope that work
  explicitly or retain assessment only; do not hide it behind a quick-package promise.
- If the client will not supply operating evidence, deliver a limited document
  review with unknowns. Do not claim functioning arrangements.
- If tracking duplicates existing work, use the existing records and simplify the
  proposed procedure before adding software.
- If records are incomplete or show severe deterioration, surface the evidence
  promptly for professional action. Preparing documents must not delay that action.

## Not included

No automatic adequacy certificate, liability opinion, CTU service, employee
surveillance, automatic questionnaire dispatch, background monitoring, Model 231
certification or production of backdated evidence. No universal scoring system or
large-company control hierarchy. These exclusions preserve the scope of this
specific proposed delivery, rather than deciding what future separate products
could support.
