# Vera treasury forecast implementation plan

Goal: ship an end-to-end, file-based treasury forecast and update workflow for
Vera. Agenzia downloading is a future acquisition route into the same invoice
import boundary. Business planning is not a dependency.

## Agreed product boundary

Only clients supplying the defined inputs qualify. No generic PDF extraction,
invented payment dates, automatic tax calculation or autonomous payment execution.
The first release covers one company, EUR, a fixed declared bank-account set and
daily closing cash for a selected horizon of at most 366 days. It does not promise
intraday liquidity or consolidation of companies/currencies.

Codex orchestrates intake and semantic review. Prepared scripts validate stable
formats and identities, perform exact arithmetic, maintain versions and render
reviewable outputs. Mechanical rules are justified by directly checkable money,
date, reference and persistence invariants, not by a general preference for
determinism. Ambiguous commercial meaning remains a model/professional judgment.

## Implementation stages

1. **Input contract and fixtures.** Fixed CSV headers or matching XLSX sheets for
   accounts, bank movements, open items, additional planned flows, settlement
   allocations and non-cash adjustments. A small manifest generated by Codex binds
   company, cutoff, horizon, coverage, sources and optional prior accepted record.
   Optional FatturaPA files use the existing parser as supporting evidence;
   invoice payment terms never establish an unpaid balance. Include first-run,
   second-run and third-run fixtures.
2. **Calculation and update engine.** Exact-cent money, daily and weekly outputs,
   bank balance bridge, partial settlements, non-cash changes, new items,
   unexplained disappearances, explicit replacement of planned items by invoices,
   retained/expired assumptions, comparable-period change explanations and
   separately identified horizon extension. Alternative dates affect a separate
   scenario without overwriting the baseline.
3. **Review and persistence.** Draft and accepted records, immutable version
   history, source and predecessor digests, same-client checks, idempotent retries,
   stale-review rejection and concurrent-write protection. A local browser review
   lets the commercialista edit dates and basis, compare previous/current figures,
   save partial work and accept the exact recomputed forecast. Mechanical errors
   cannot be waived by a review click.
4. **Deliverables and archive.** Studio Archive input/run binding; canonical JSON,
   daily/weekly/event/change CSVs, XLSX workbook, human-readable report and usable
   HTML review. Preserve declared model-context boundaries and produce the
   archive's model-data report and final artifact declarations.
5. **Vera integration.** Module manifest, canonical skill and Vera routing skill,
   workflow catalog and generated registry, components, runtime recipe, privacy
   manifest, localized process page and Vera navigation. Source instructions must
   say exactly which files work and how the professional reviews the result.
6. **Validation and release.** Adversarial and normal fixtures, full CLI/archive
   lifecycle, browser save/reopen/apply, report numeric readback, relevant quality
   gates and package tests. Rebuild affected packages from source. Follow the
   repository PR, CI and deployment workflow and verify published artifacts when
   released. Do not call synthetic cases real-client validation.

## Acceptance examples

- First forecast ends September at EUR 15,000; next update at EUR -16,000 after
  EUR 20,000 delayed receipts, EUR 3,000 credit note and EUR 8,000 new payment.
- An early-collection alternative shows its own result and preserves baseline.
- A third run settles the residual and removes the past receipt from the future
  forecast without duplicating bank cash.
- Missing required tables, duplicate stable keys, altered identity, inconsistent
  balances, reused allocations, changed sources and stale decisions fail visibly.
- A credit note is not counted as cash; a disappearing row is not called paid
  without sufficient settlement/adjustment evidence.
- A planned cost replaced by an invoice produces one expected payment.
- Reviewed dates survive an evidenced partial payment, but expired dates or
  changed payment terms return to review.
- Fresh sources and prior accepted records remain separately inspectable.

The real browser check used a synthetic Studio Archive case. Moving the full
C101 receipt to 2 October changed the minimum daily cash from EUR 15,000 to
EUR -35,000; reload retained the date and acceptance retained the reviewer and
conclusion. A separate earlier-date alternative returned EUR 15,000 without
changing the accepted baseline. The distinct partial-payment example above is
covered by the three-run engine tests. Neither is real-studio adoption evidence.

Work is isolated in `codex/vera-treasury`; unrelated main-checkout changes are
outside this task. No Agenzia live downloading is included in this release.
