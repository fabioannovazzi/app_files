# Three document-led pilot exercises

Status: protocol prepared; independent professional acceptance not yet completed.
Use fictional companies, people and acts only. The current automated tests use
prequalified assumptions and do not demonstrate legal interpretation from raw files.

For each exercise retain the actual conversation, documents and reviewed
extractions, proposed findings, professional corrections, T0 and later dossier,
open questions, source/version log and measured human review sessions. The reviewer
must be able to trace each accepted conclusion to the original document. Record
time separately for intake, subjects, eligibility, obstacles, premiums, events,
dossier and rework. Leave unmeasured time unknown. Agree acceptable review effort
with the studio; do not invent a profitability threshold.

1. **Positive:** prepare internally consistent fictional corporate and economic
   records, complete relevant-person information and source-bound evidence for the
   mandatory controls. Include a declared premium unsupported by its attachment:
   it must remain unverified until the reviewer sees adequate evidence. The
   expected outcome is documentary readiness only after actual recorded review,
   with no official rating or permission to submit.
2. **Incomplete:** omit a relevant-person document and the occurrence date of an
   event, while giving a later knowledge date. Start through ordinary conversation.
   Expect a targeted request, unresolved scope and an urgent unqualified calendar
   entry, without treating the knowledge date as the deadline origin. Add the
   missing document/date in a later version; retain T0 and measure rework.
3. **Ineligible:** provide a clearly documented failed access condition selected
   and legally qualified by the professional. Expect a reasoned negative dossier
   before investing in premium work, without promising a remedy. Include an
   independently reviewed pending-application event requiring communication,
   with a late externally supplied receipt. The calendar must distinguish that
   lateness from an AGCM sanction decision and retain the record.

Across all three, check the engagement split (company declarations and signature;
studio investigation and drafts), the synthetic notice/retention plan and scoped
access policy. A real pilot may begin only after the studio has reviewed those
prerequisites for the actual people, legal authority and selected model account.
Test instruments do not constitute signed mandates, valid notices or consent.
