# Fictional accounting case: Officina Arco

All companies, identifiers and documents are fictional. Currency EUR; amounts use a decimal point and no thousands separator. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Account 1000 is the only bank account in scope, 1400 input VAT, 2100 trade payables and 6100 services. Journal debits and credits are positive amounts in separate columns. Bank amounts are positive for receipts and negative for payments. Each Entry groups accounting lines; Document is the XML invoice number and Reference is the bank transaction reference.

The March journal contains three supplier invoices: DEMO-001 EUR 1,220; DEMO-002 EUR 732; DEMO-003 EUR 488. Invoice XML files carry the same numbers and figures. DEMO-001 and DEMO-002 were paid in March, with exact transaction references TRN-001 and TRN-002. DEMO-003 was paid on 4 April, outside the March cut-off. The reported-open-items export is an earlier population still listing all three documents; reconcile its reported amounts against evidence at the selected cut-off rather than treating it as current unpaid balances.

At 1 March the bank balance was EUR 6,000. March movements total EUR -1,952 and the closing balance is EUR 4,048. No additional bank accounts or March movements exist in this fictional case. The April update adds payment of EUR 488, reducing the balance to EUR 3,560. The March journal has 13 lines in five entries; total debits and credits are each EUR 4,392. The April journal has 15 lines in six entries and totals EUR 4,880 on each side. No accounting or professional review is implied by these declared teaching facts.
